    Notable results

    tulip_rs_go beats cinar on 45 of 45 compared indicators (cinar is wired where it is param-compatible; the remaining indicators have no pure-Go competitor run). Median speedup: 2,283× — cinar v2's goroutine/channel stream API pays a per-bar scheduling cost the cgo path does not.

    Largest wins vs cinar:

    Indicator Speedup vs cinar
    emv 7,491×
    nvi 6,601×

    qstick 4,833×
    wcprice 4,762×
