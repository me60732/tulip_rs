    Notable results

    tulip_rs_node beats every JS competitor on 23 compared indicators against technicalindicators and 36 compared indicators against indicatorts. Median speedup: 57.33x vs technicalindicators Median speedup: 7.00x vs indicatorts

    Largest wins vs technicalindicators (pure JS, loop-heavy implementations):

    wma 380.84x
